AIR ATTACK

Dragon funnel

Guide dragons into the core instead of letting them circle the outside.

  1. Scout air defenses and sweepers.
  2. Clear both sides to shape a funnel.
  3. Send your main army toward the center.
  4. Time spells around heavy defenses.
🪨
GROUND ATTACK

Core push

Reliable pathing matters more than deploying every troop at once.

  1. Identify high-value defenses.
  2. Clear the outer buildings.
  3. Open a path toward the core.
  4. Support the push with spells.
🏆
WAR STRATEGY

Scout first

Plan around the layout, enemy heroes and possible traps.

  1. Inspect the enemy base.
  2. Choose a realistic attack route.
  3. Plan hero abilities and spell timing.
  4. Practice your entry and funnel.

Keep practicing

These are general guides, not guaranteed three-star strategies. Adapt each attack to your Town Hall and available troops.
