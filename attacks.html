AIR ATTACKS

Dragon funnel

Guide dragons toward the base core instead of letting them drift around the outside.

  1. Scout air defenses and sweepers.
  2. Clear buildings on both sides to shape a funnel.
  3. Send the main group toward the center.
  4. Time your spells around high-damage defenses.
🪨
GROUND ATTACKS

Core push

A good ground attack starts with pathing and a reliable entry.

  1. Identify your target core defenses.
  2. Clear the outside buildings.
  3. Open a path with suitable support units.
  4. Save enough troops for cleanup.
🎯
WAR PLANNING

Three-star mindset

Think through the full attack before you deploy the first troop.

  1. Review the enemy Town Hall and defensive layout.
  2. Choose an entry and exit path.
  3. Identify spell timing and likely traps.
  4. Practice against similar layouts.
These are general fundamentals, not verified current-meta army compositions. We will expand this academy with Town Hall-specific guides and tested strategies.
